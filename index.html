SynDesk · Free classroom seating chart generator

A seating chart that respects every student's needs, in seconds.

SynDesk seats students with accommodations first, keeps peer supports close and separates pairs that shouldn't sit together, then explains every decision. Everything you enter stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Where your data lives

SynDesk has no server and no accounts. Classes, students and charts are saved in this browser on this device. To move them to another computer, or to keep a copy safe, use Download backupon the dashboard and open the file on the other device. Clearing your browser data erases what's stored here, so keep a backup.
