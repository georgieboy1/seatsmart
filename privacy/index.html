Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

SynDesk is a free classroom seating tool made by KC Studio, a Future of the Isles studio. It was built so that student information never has to leave a teacher's device. This page explains exactly what that means.

1. Your data stays in your browser

The classes, students, accommodations, relationships, layouts and charts you enter are saved in your web browser's local storage on the device you're using. SynDesk has no server-side database and no accounts. We never receive, see or store any of it.

2. Files you create

When you download a backup or export a CSV, the file is created on your device and saved wherever you choose. Those files can contain sensitive student information, so store and share them according to your school's policies.

3. No tracking

SynDesk does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or cookies. Fonts and code are served with the app itself, so using SynDesk makes no requests to third parties.

4. Hosting

The app's files are delivered by our hosting provider, which may keep standard web server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request) to operate and secure the service. These logs never include anything you type into SynDesk.

5. Deleting your data

Because everything is stored in your browser, you are in full control. Delete individual students, classes or charts in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove everything at once.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the contact form at futureoftheisles.org/about.
